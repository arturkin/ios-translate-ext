Privacy Policy — Translate Icelandic

Last updated: June 2026

Translate Icelandic is a Safari extension that translates Icelandic text into English and lets you look up individual words. This policy explains what the extension does with your data. The short version: it sends only the text you ask it to translate or look up, it does this only to fetch a result, and it keeps nothing on any server we control.

What the extension processes

  • Text you choose to translate or look up. When you translate a page or select a word or phrase, the relevant text is sent to a translation or dictionary provider to return the result. No other page content is read or transmitted.
  • Local settings and cache. Your feature toggles and a cache of recent translations are stored locally in your browser (storage.local) so repeated lookups are instant. This data stays on your device; it is never uploaded to us, and we operate no server that receives it.

Where text is sent

To perform a translation or lookup, the text is sent directly from your browser to one of these third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Microsoft Azure Translator — translation.
  • MyMemory (Translated Labs) — translation, used as a keyless fallback.
  • ylhyra.is / BÍN (The Árni Magnússon Institute) — Icelandic inflections, fetched only when you open a word's inflection table.

These requests are made over HTTPS. We do not add identifiers to them, and we receive no copy of them. The word look-up panel also offers a link to the Glosbe dictionary (glosbe.com); opening that link is an ordinary web page visit you initiate — the extension does not send your data to Glosbe.

What the extension does NOT do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising.
  • No user accounts, profiles, or sign-in.
  • No collection of browsing history, and no reading of pages you don't translate.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone.

Permissions

The extension requests broad host access only so it can offer translation on any website you visit and reach the translation and dictionary services above. It does not use that access for any other purpose.
